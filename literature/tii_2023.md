# Seok, Tan, Cai & Park — Digital-Twin Consistency Checking (TII 2023)

* IEEE Trans. Industrial Informatics 19(4), 2023, DOI 10.1109/TII.2022.3200598.
* Full-text status: **NOT_ESTABLISHED**. IEEE Xplore is blocked and no legal
  open-access copy was located. Only metadata/abstract-level facts are used.
* Known scope (from title/abstract): digital-twin consistency checking in
  smart manufacturing based on **observed timed events** with **unobservable
  transitions**; the industrial example is a USB manufacturing system; timed
  coloured Petri nets (TCPN) are used to model concurrency of production,
  transport and equipment.

## Role in this evaluation

Industrial motivation only. A TCPN → ordinary P/T TPN extraction is **not**
attempted because (a) the full model is not available to us, (b) colour
semantics and data-dependent guards do not project faithfully onto a plain
P/T TPN, and (c) the task explicitly allows keeping it as industrial
application evidence rather than forcing a conversion. Any claim about its
SCG partitioning or observed timed events would be NOT_ESTABLISHED without
the full text, and is therefore not made.
